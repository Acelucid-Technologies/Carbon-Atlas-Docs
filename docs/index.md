# Carbon Atlas Documentation

Carbon Atlas is a platform that helps organizations measure, track, and report
their carbon emissions in line with industry and regulatory standards. It brings
together emissions calculations, compliance tracking, and reporting into a single
system, so businesses can understand their environmental impact and meet
sustainability requirements with confidence.

This site is a starting point for anyone who wants to understand how the
platform works, whether you're a partner, a new team member, or simply
curious about the system.
